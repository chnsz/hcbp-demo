# Introduction

## What is Media Processing Center (MPC)

Media Processing Center (MPC) is a one-stop media processing service provided by Huawei Cloud for the production, processing, and distribution of audio and video content. It offers capabilities such as transcoding, screenshot, watermarking, video encryption, and audio extraction. Built on elastic cloud computing resources, MPC helps users quickly convert uploaded source audio and video files into output formats suitable for different terminals and network conditions, without the need to build and maintain transcoding clusters.

MPC supports a variety of mainstream audio and video codecs and container formats, and provides both preset transcoding templates and custom transcoding templates. Users can either use system templates to complete transcoding quickly or flexibly define the output format, audio parameters, and video parameters according to business requirements. In addition, MPC supports transcoding template groups, transcoding tasks, and transcoding message notifications, making it easy to integrate media processing workflows into existing business systems.

With MPC, enterprises can significantly reduce the development and O&M costs of audio and video processing and improve content distribution efficiency, making it widely applicable to scenarios such as online education, video on demand, live streaming replay, and enterprise training.
