# Introduction

## What is Enterprise Project Management Service (EPS)

Enterprise Project Management Service (EPS) is an enterprise-level resource management service provided by Huawei Cloud, allowing enterprises to group, authorize, and perform cost accounting on cloud resources by project. With enterprise projects, enterprises can divide resources of different businesses, departments, or environments into isolated projects, achieving logical isolation and unified management of resources to meet the management requirements of multi-business and multi-team collaboration scenarios.

EPS provides full lifecycle management capabilities for enterprise projects, including creation, modification, enabling, disabling, and deletion, and supports integration with enterprise organizational structures and Identity and Access Management (IAM) policies to achieve fine-grained permission control by project. In addition, enterprise projects can work with Cost Center to help enterprises perform independent accounting and cost allocation for resource consumption of different projects.

With enterprise projects, enterprises can establish clear resource ownership, simplify resource retrieval and O&M management, and lay a solid foundation for subsequent compliance auditing, cost optimization, and automated governance.
