# Introduction

## What is Enterprise Project Management Service (EPS)

Enterprise Project Management Service (EPS) is an enterprise-level resource management service provided by Huawei Cloud, designed to help enterprises plan, classify, and authorize cloud resources by project. With enterprise projects, enterprises can group resources of different businesses, departments, or environments into independent projects, enabling grouped resource management, cost aggregation, and permission isolation, thereby improving the standardization and efficiency of cloud resource management.

EPS supports the full lifecycle management of enterprise projects, including creation, enabling, disabling, and deletion, and can work with Identity and Access Management (IAM) to grant fine-grained access permissions to users and user groups at the enterprise project level. Enterprise projects can also collaborate with capabilities such as tags and cost centers to help enterprises achieve resource classification, cost allocation, and compliance auditing.

With EPS, enterprises can build a clear multi-project resource management architecture, laying a solid foundation for subsequent resource governance, cost optimization, and operations management.
